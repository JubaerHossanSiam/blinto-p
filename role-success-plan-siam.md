# Role Success Plan — Siam

## Role

**Senior Frontend Developer**

**Core department:** Engineering  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Deliver high-quality frontend experiences while providing strong ownership, technical judgment, and support that raises the reliability and capability of the frontend team.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Frontend quality** — interfaces are accurate, responsive, accessible, performant, and consistent with approved designs.
2. **Independent ownership** — complex frontend work is understood, planned, implemented, tested, and closed with minimal unnecessary supervision.
3. **Engineering judgment** — solutions are maintainable, reusable where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary complexity or technical debt.
4. **Quality assurance** — browser, device, responsive, interaction, and regression issues are identified before handoff.
5. **Team contribution** — other frontend developers receive useful support, review, knowledge sharing, and practical technical guidance.
6. **Delivery awareness** — estimates, blockers, dependencies, and risks are communicated early enough for the team to act.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include implementation accuracy, low preventable rework, successful launches, performance improvements, reusable components, technical review, mentoring, issue prevention, documentation, and dependable delivery.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Siam consistently delivers complex frontend work at a high standard, anticipates quality and technical risks, improves reusable engineering practices, and makes other developers more effective.

**Effective** performance means assigned work is delivered reliably to the expected frontend standard, risks are communicated, quality checks are performed, and collaboration with design and engineering is dependable.

**Needs Improvement** applies when preventable UI defects, responsive issues, avoidable rework, weak testing, late escalation, or poor implementation discipline repeatedly affect delivery.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
