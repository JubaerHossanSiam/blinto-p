# Role Success Plan — Imran

## Role

**Product Designer**

**Core department:** Design  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Design product experiences that connect user needs, product strategy, usability, business goals, and implementation feasibility into coherent, scalable solutions.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Product thinking** — design work starts from the user problem, product objective, and success criteria rather than isolated screens.
2. **UX quality** — flows, information architecture, states, interactions, and edge cases are clear and usable.
3. **Visual quality** — product interfaces are polished, consistent, accessible, and aligned with the product's design language.
4. **System thinking** — reusable patterns, components, and design-system conventions are used and improved to support scale.
5. **Cross-functional collaboration** — product, engineering, growth, and other stakeholders are aligned early enough to avoid avoidable rework.
6. **Outcome orientation** — design decisions are connected to adoption, conversion, usability, retention, efficiency, or another agreed product outcome.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include complete product flows, strong interaction states, reduced design/development rework, design-system contributions, product or user feedback, successful launches, usability improvements, and measurable product outcomes.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Imran consistently turns ambiguous product problems into clear, scalable experiences, anticipates edge cases, strengthens the product system, and materially improves user or business outcomes through design.

**Effective** performance means product work is thoughtful, complete, implementation-ready, aligned with requirements, and demonstrates sound user and product judgment.

**Needs Improvement** applies when work focuses mainly on surface visuals, important states or flows are missed, product rationale is weak, handoff creates avoidable rework, or feedback is not translated into better decisions.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
