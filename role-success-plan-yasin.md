# Role Success Plan — Yasin

## Role

**Frontend Developer**

**Core department:** Engineering  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Deliver reliable frontend implementation from approved designs and requirements while improving independence, quality control, and technical consistency over time.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Implementation accuracy** — work matches approved design, content, and functional expectations.
2. **Responsive behavior** — interfaces work correctly across required screen sizes, devices, and supported browsers.
3. **Maintainable implementation** — code follows team standards and avoids unnecessary duplication or fragility.
4. **Self-QA** — common visual, interaction, content, and regression issues are caught before review.
5. **Delivery discipline** — progress and blockers are communicated early and tasks are followed through to completion.
6. **Development in capability** — feedback is applied consistently and increasingly complex tasks are handled with less supervision.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include implementation accuracy, responsive and browser quality, rework levels, code review feedback, successful releases, dependable task completion, and visible improvement over time.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Yasin consistently delivers polished frontend work, detects issues before review, learns quickly from feedback, and increasingly handles complex work independently.

**Effective** performance means assigned work meets requirements, passes expected quality checks, is delivered reliably, and feedback is incorporated into later work.

**Needs Improvement** applies when preventable defects recur, testing is weak, rework remains high, blockers are surfaced late, or the same feedback repeatedly appears.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
