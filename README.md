# Blinto Team Performance Framework

The Blinto Team Performance Framework defines how Blinto sets expectations, measures performance, conducts monthly reviews, and supports career growth.

## Core components

- Universal Career Framework (L1–L7)
- Department Career Ladders
- Role Success Plans
- 100-Point KPI Framework
- Task-level KPI measurement in ClickUp
- Monthly KPI Reviews
- Growth & Development
- Role Excellence

## Performance philosophy

Blinto measures observable work and outcomes wherever possible. Task-level evidence is collected continuously in ClickUp, while monthly reviews add the areas that require broader managerial judgment.

The framework is designed to be transparent, evidence-based, and focused on continuous improvement.

## Source of truth

- **GitHub:** methodology, definitions, career framework, scoring rules
- **ClickUp:** work execution, task-level KPI data, monthly KPI reviews
