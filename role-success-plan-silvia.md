# Role Success Plan — Silvia

## Role

**UI/UX Designer**

**Core department:** Design  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Create clear, usable, conversion-aware digital experiences that solve real user and business problems and can be implemented effectively by the development team.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Design quality** — interfaces are visually clear, consistent, polished, and appropriate to the product or brand.
2. **UX thinking** — design decisions reflect user needs, task flow, information hierarchy, usability, and business goals rather than aesthetics alone.
3. **Conversion awareness** — relevant experiences consider clarity, trust, friction, calls to action, and measurable user outcomes.
4. **Developer handoff** — designs, states, responsive behavior, assets, and specifications are complete enough for efficient implementation.
5. **Design systems discipline** — reusable patterns and components are applied consistently and improved when needed.
6. **Iteration quality** — feedback, user insight, business context, and implementation constraints are incorporated constructively without losing design intent.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include design quality, reduced handoff ambiguity, usability improvements, client or stakeholder feedback, developer rework levels, design-system contributions, conversion-related improvements, and successful launches.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Silvia consistently produces thoughtful, high-quality experiences, improves usability and conversion outcomes, strengthens reusable design systems, and makes implementation easier through excellent handoff and collaboration.

**Effective** performance means design work meets agreed requirements, demonstrates sound UX judgment, is implementation-ready, and responds appropriately to feedback and constraints.

**Needs Improvement** applies when designs are visually or functionally inconsistent, UX rationale is weak, handoff creates avoidable rework, responsive or interaction states are incomplete, or recurring feedback is not incorporated.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
