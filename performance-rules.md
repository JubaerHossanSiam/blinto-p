# Performance System Rules

These rules explain how Blinto performance evidence is collected, validated, and used. They apply to everyone using the system.

## 1. Only eligible work becomes performance evidence

A task counts toward task-based performance only when it is an eligible **Task**, is completed, contains the required KPI rating fields, and the rating comes from an authorized reviewer.

Use **Task** for a meaningful individual deliverable. Use **Feature** for supporting or internal work breakdown. Features remain trackable in ClickUp but do not independently enter task-rating coverage or task-based KPI scoring.

## 2. Rating authority must be valid

A rating is not valid simply because someone can edit the ClickUp task.

The reviewer must have a legitimate authority relationship for that employee and task. The system checks the rating actor and audit history before accepting the value as verified performance evidence.

- An employee cannot validate or rate their own work.
- An unauthorized peer rating is invalid.
- A person outside the employee's approved review authority cannot create a verified rating merely by editing the KPI fields.
- Where Blinto has an explicit reviewer-authority map, that map takes precedence over a generic hierarchy assumption.

## 4. Exceptions do not silently become valid

Sometimes a CEO/Admin or another person outside the normal authority path may need to review a specific task.

An outside-authority rating does **not** automatically become verified. It enters **Needs Validation** and may be explicitly **Validated** or **Rejected** for that task. Validating one exception does not grant that person permanent rating authority.

## 5. Validation states

**Verified** — the task is eligible, the rating is complete, and the reviewer/authority relationship is valid.

**Needs Validation** — the evidence or rating exists, but an authority exception or another validation check requires review before it can count as verified.

**Invalid** — the rating fails a rule, such as self-rating or an unauthorized peer rating, and must not count toward performance scoring.

If an unauthorized change is made after a verified rating, the system should preserve the last verified value rather than replacing trusted evidence with the unauthorized edit.

## 6. Ratings must be evidence-based

Review the agreed brief, acceptance criteria, deadline, employee responsibility, actual deliverable, task updates, and relevant stakeholder feedback.

Rate demonstrated work — not personality, seniority, effort alone, or personal preference.

**Effective** means the agreed expectation was met. Ratings above or below Effective require observable evidence. A field with no valid observation should remain blank; blank does not mean zero or poor performance.

Use the [Task Rating Guide](/task-rating-guide) for field-by-field standards.

## 7. ClickUp evidence is not rescored during the monthly review

ClickUp-derived KPI evidence is aggregated by the performance system. Managers do not manually rescore those same task-based KPIs during the monthly review.

HRMS-derived attendance and leave/policy values are also system evidence and become read-only when connected.

## 8. Growth & Development and Role Excellence are monthly ratings

**Growth & Development** and **Role Excellence** are manager-rated during the monthly review, not on ordinary ClickUp tasks.

Growth & Development uses progress against agreed development goals and demonstrated capability improvement.

Role Excellence uses documented monthly outcomes against the employee's Role Success Plan.

Existing ClickUp KPI Review tasks are not used to score these two fields.

## 9. Rating coverage must be complete before finalization

Completed eligible Tasks form the task-rating coverage denominator.

Monthly performance should not be finalized while eligible completed tasks are still missing required KPI review evidence. The target is **100% rating coverage** for eligible completed tasks.

## 10. Monthly review flow

The monthly review moves through:

**Draft → Submitted → Finalized**

Managers submit the monthly manager-rated fields and feedback by the **29th**. CEO/Admin reviews exceptions and calibration on the **30th/31st**. The finalized monthly result is available on the **1st**.

The final result combines system evidence and the authorized monthly review. It should not be changed through informal or duplicate scoring.

## 11. Employees can challenge incorrect evidence

Employees may provide missing context or point out factual errors.

When evidence is wrong, correct the source evidence or validation state. Do not compensate for an incorrect task rating by changing an unrelated KPI or monthly score.

## The principle

**Performance should come from attributable work, observable evidence, authorized review, and a traceable validation history.**

Visibility does not equal rating authority. Editing access does not equal validation authority.
