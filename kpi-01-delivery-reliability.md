# KPI 01 — Delivery & Reliability

## Points

**10 points**

## Measurement

**Delivery Status and Delay / Blockage Responsibility**

## Primary evaluator

Delivery Lead / responsible reviewer

## Definition

Measures reliable completion of agreed deliverables. Client and third-party delays should not punish the employee. The Employee / Assignee value identifies delays attributable to the assignee.

## Universal rating scale

| Rating | Score |
|---|---:|
| Exceptional | 5 |
| Strong | 4 |
| Effective | 3 |
| Needs Improvement | 2 |
| Significant Improvement Needed | 1 |

For a 10-point KPI using a five-point rating:

`Rating ÷ 5 × 10 = KPI score`

## Evidence standard

Use observable evidence from work, deliverables, outcomes, or agreed development goals. Avoid scoring based solely on personality or general impression.
