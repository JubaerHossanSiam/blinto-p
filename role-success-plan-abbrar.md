# Role Success Plan — Abbrar

## Role

**SEO Executive**

**Core department:** Growth (Sales & Marketing)  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Execute dependable SEO work that improves organic visibility, technical health, content performance, and measurable client or business outcomes.

## Core success outcomes

1. **SEO execution quality** — assigned technical, on-page, content, local, or off-page work is completed accurately and according to agreed priorities.
2. **Technical discipline** — issues are identified, documented, prioritized, and followed through with appropriate validation after implementation.
3. **Search intent alignment** — keyword, content, and page recommendations reflect user intent and business relevance rather than search volume alone.
4. **Measurement and reporting** — organic performance is monitored using appropriate evidence and changes are explained clearly.
5. **Reliable delivery** — SEO tasks, audits, recommendations, and follow-ups are completed on time with blockers communicated early.
6. **Learning and improvement** — feedback, algorithm changes, tools, AI-assisted workflows, and performance data are used to improve future SEO work.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include completed audits, implemented fixes, ranking or visibility gains, qualified organic traffic, indexed pages, technical issue resolution, content improvements, reporting quality, client outcomes, and reduced repeat errors.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Abbrar consistently executes high-quality SEO work, connects findings to business impact, spots meaningful opportunities proactively, validates outcomes, and demonstrates rapidly increasing independence and judgment.

**Effective** performance means assigned SEO work is accurate, timely, evidence-based, appropriately documented, and contributes to agreed organic-growth goals.

**Needs Improvement** applies when recommendations are generic or inaccurate, implementation validation is weak, tasks require repeated follow-up, reporting lacks useful interpretation, or recurring errors are not corrected through learning.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
