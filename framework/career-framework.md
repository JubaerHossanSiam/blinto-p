# Career Framework

## Universal Level Framework

| Level | Rank Name | Core Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate | Entry / Foundation |
| L2 | Executive / Specialist | Independent Contributor |
| L3 | Senior | Strong Ownership |
| L4 | Lead | Leads Work or Small Team |
| L5 | Principal (IC) / Manager (Mgmt) | High Impact |
| L6 | Architect (IC) / Head (Mgmt) | Department Authority |
| L7 | Director | Strategic Leadership |

## Dual Career Track

At L4 and above, employees may grow through two paths:

- Individual Contributor (IC Track)
- Management Track

Both tracks are equal in level and salary band.

## Role Success Plans

Every full-time role should have a Role Success Plan defining what excellent performance means for that role.

Role Excellence in the monthly KPI review is assessed against that role-specific plan.
