# Framework Overview

## Purpose

The Blinto Team Performance Framework provides a common language for performance expectations across the company.

It connects:

1. Career level
2. Role expectations
3. Actual work
4. KPI evidence
5. Monthly review
6. Development

## Measurement architecture

Employee work happens in ClickUp.

↓

Relevant task-level KPI fields are completed.

↓

Monthly performance data is aggregated.

↓

The employee's KPI Review captures Growth & Development and Role Excellence.

↓

The employee receives a 100-point performance score.

## Principles

- Measure outcomes, not activity alone.
- Prefer observable evidence over subjective impressions.
- Separate task-level evidence from monthly assessment.
- Do not punish employees for delays caused by clients or third parties.
- Make expectations visible.
- Use reviews to improve performance, not merely to rank people.
