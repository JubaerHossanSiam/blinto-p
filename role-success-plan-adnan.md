# Adnan — Role Success Plan

**Working Role:** Content Writer / SEO Content Contributor
**Core Department:** Growth (Marketing)
**Career Level:** To be confirmed
**Assessment Scope:** Standard Blinto Team Performance Framework

> The working role above is based on Adnan's current ClickUp work across Blinto, RLA, and client content tasks, including website content outlines, service-page content, SaaS/industry content, and SEO-oriented deliverables. Formal title and career level should be confirmed separately.

## Role Purpose

Create clear, accurate, useful, search-aware content that supports Blinto, partner, and client growth goals while maintaining quality, brand alignment, and dependable delivery.

## Core Success Outcomes

### 1. Content Quality

Produce well-structured, accurate, readable content that matches the brief, audience, brand voice, and intended business purpose.

**Observable evidence:** reviewer feedback, acceptance quality, editing/rework required, factual accuracy, clarity, structure, and consistency with the brief.

### 2. Search & Intent Alignment

Create content that addresses the intended search or buyer intent and supports SEO, GEO, service-page, or campaign objectives where applicable.

**Observable evidence:** alignment with content outlines, keyword/topic intent, internal-linking or on-page requirements, SERP/content research, and relevance to the target audience.

### 3. Reliable Delivery

Complete assigned content deliverables according to agreed deadlines and keep work status visible throughout the production process.

**Observable evidence:** on-time completion, ClickUp updates, early communication of blockers, predictable handoff, and limited avoidable delays.

### 4. Research & Subject Understanding

Build enough understanding of the subject, client, product, and audience to write credible content rather than producing generic copy.

**Observable evidence:** research quality, use of relevant source material, accurate terminology, understanding of offers and audiences, and reduced factual corrections during review.

### 5. Collaboration & Revision Quality

Work effectively with strategists, SEO, delivery, design, and other stakeholders; incorporate feedback accurately and improve content efficiently.

**Observable evidence:** responsiveness to feedback, revision accuracy, collaboration on outlines/briefs, low repeated-error rate, and effective handoff.

### 6. Business-Oriented Content Contribution

Understand that content is created to support outcomes such as visibility, engagement, qualified traffic, conversion, authority, or client value—not only to complete a writing task.

**Observable evidence:** stronger calls to action, better positioning, contribution to service/product messaging, useful content ideas, and demonstrated awareness of business goals.

## Role Excellence Assessment

KPI 10 — **Role Excellence** should evaluate Adnan against the six outcomes above using documented evidence from the review month.

### Exceptional — 5

Consistently produces high-quality, strategically useful content with minimal revision, demonstrates strong research and audience understanding, and contributes ideas or improvements beyond assigned briefs.

### Strong — 4

Frequently exceeds expected quality, delivers reliably, responds well to feedback, and demonstrates good understanding of search intent and business objectives.

### Effective — 3

Reliably meets role expectations, completes assigned content to an acceptable standard, follows briefs, and collaborates appropriately through review and revision.

### Needs Improvement — 2

Performance is inconsistent in quality, research, delivery, brief adherence, revisions, or communication, resulting in recurring rework or avoidable delays.

### Significant Improvement Needed — 1

Regularly falls below essential role expectations and requires substantial improvement in writing quality, reliability, collaboration, or understanding of the assignment.

## Development Goals

Agree 2–3 specific development goals for each assessment period. Goals should focus on measurable improvements such as SEO/content strategy, research depth, conversion writing, product/service understanding, editing quality, or independent content ownership and should be reviewed monthly under KPI 9 — Growth & Development.
