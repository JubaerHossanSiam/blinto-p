# KPI 10 — Role Excellence

## Points

**10 points**

## Measurement

**Monthly KPI Review dropdown, 1–5**

## Primary evaluator

Manager / reviewer

## Definition

Measures performance against the employee's role-specific Role Success Plan. The scoring scale is universal while the evidence and targets are role-specific.

## Universal rating scale

| Rating | Score |
|---|---:|
| Exceptional | 5 |
| Strong | 4 |
| Effective | 3 |
| Needs Improvement | 2 |
| Significant Improvement Needed | 1 |

For a 10-point KPI using a five-point rating:

`Rating ÷ 5 × 10 = KPI score`

## Evidence standard

Use observable evidence from work, deliverables, outcomes, or agreed development goals. Avoid scoring based solely on personality or general impression.
