## Monthly review contents

- Review the eight task-based KPIs
- Assess Growth & Development
- Assess Role Excellence
- Record key achievements
- Record challenges / blockers
- Manager feedback
- Employee reflection
- Action items
- Next-month priorities
- Complete the 1:1 meeting

## Employee reflection

The employee should answer:

- What went well?
- What was challenging?
- What did I learn?
- What support do I need?
- What will I improve next month?

## Review principle

The monthly review should summarize evidence already collected during the month rather than reconstructing performance from memory.
