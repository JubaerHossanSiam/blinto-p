# Role Success Plan — Jannatul Mukta

## Role

**People & Operations Executive**

**Core department:** People, Operations & Finance  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Keep Blinto's people and day-to-day operations organized, dependable, documented, and easy for the team to navigate.

## Core success outcomes

1. **People operations accuracy** — employee records, attendance, leave, onboarding, offboarding, and recurring HR administration are accurate and timely.
2. **Operational reliability** — recurring office and administrative responsibilities are completed without avoidable follow-up from leadership.
3. **Employee support** — team questions and routine people issues are handled respectfully, promptly, and through the correct process.
4. **Process discipline** — policies, checklists, records, and SOPs are maintained so recurring work does not depend on memory.
5. **Follow-through** — assigned actions, approvals, renewals, purchases, and operational dependencies are tracked until closure.
6. **Improvement mindset** — repeated friction or administrative waste is identified and simplified through better workflows, tools, or documentation.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include accurate attendance and leave records, timely onboarding/offboarding, complete documentation, closed operational actions, reduced follow-up, employee response quality, policy implementation, vendor/admin coordination, and process improvements.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Mukta runs people and operational routines with high accuracy and independence, anticipates needs, reduces leadership follow-up, and continuously improves how the company operates.

**Effective** performance means recurring responsibilities are completed accurately and on time, records remain organized, employees receive dependable support, and assigned actions are followed through.

**Needs Improvement** applies when records are unreliable, recurring work requires repeated chasing, deadlines or follow-ups are missed, employee requests are left unresolved, or preventable administrative errors recur.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
