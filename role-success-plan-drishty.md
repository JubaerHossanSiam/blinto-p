# Role Success Plan — Drishty

## Role

**Content Strategist**

**Core department:** Growth (Sales & Marketing)  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Create and guide content that strengthens Blinto's positioning, supports growth priorities, communicates expertise clearly, and turns market insight into useful audience-facing assets.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Strategic relevance** — content priorities are connected to target audiences, offers, campaigns, SEO/GEO goals, or authority-building objectives.
2. **Content quality** — content is clear, accurate, useful, well-structured, and appropriate to the intended channel and audience.
3. **Subject-matter depth** — research and collaboration produce credible content rather than generic surface-level material.
4. **Distribution readiness** — core ideas can be adapted effectively across website, social, email, video, or other relevant channels.
5. **Consistency and delivery** — agreed content is planned, progressed, reviewed, and completed reliably.
6. **Performance learning** — engagement, search, lead, audience, or campaign evidence is used to improve future content decisions.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include published assets, content calendars, campaign support, organic visibility, engagement, qualified traffic or leads, content reuse, stakeholder feedback, research quality, and documented performance learning.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Drishty consistently produces differentiated, credible content that strengthens Blinto's authority, supports measurable growth outcomes, and creates reusable ideas across channels.

**Effective** performance means content is useful, aligned with agreed priorities, delivered reliably, appropriately researched, and improved based on feedback and performance evidence.

**Needs Improvement** applies when content is generic, weakly researched, disconnected from audience or business priorities, repeatedly late, difficult to repurpose, or produced without learning from results.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
