# Role Success Plan — Ifrat

## Role

**Project Manager / Website & Growth Lead**

**Core department:** Project Delivery  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Own reliable delivery for the Website & Growth team by turning client and internal commitments into clear plans, coordinated execution, strong communication, and measurable outcomes.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Delivery reliability** — projects and recurring work are planned, assigned, tracked, and delivered on time; risks and dependencies are surfaced early.
2. **Client confidence** — clients receive clear, timely communication, realistic expectations, and dependable follow-through.
3. **Team coordination** — developers, designers, SEO, content, and other contributors understand priorities, owners, due dates, and blockers.
4. **Quality control** — deliverables meet agreed scope and quality before client handoff and avoid preventable rework.
5. **Commercial awareness** — delivery decisions consider scope, effort, profitability, retention, and opportunities to create additional client value.
6. **Operational improvement** — recurring delivery problems are converted into better processes, templates, checklists, or SOPs.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include on-time delivery, blocker handling, client feedback, reduced rework, accurate task planning, clean ClickUp ownership, successful launches, retained accounts, scope control, and documented process improvements.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Ifrat consistently creates a predictable delivery environment, anticipates risks before they become problems, strengthens client trust, improves team execution, and materially improves delivery systems beyond managing day-to-day tasks.

**Effective** performance means agreed work is planned and coordinated reliably, stakeholders stay informed, blockers are handled appropriately, and delivery quality meets expectations.

**Needs Improvement** applies when avoidable delays, unclear ownership, weak follow-up, poor escalation, preventable client surprises, or recurring coordination failures materially affect delivery.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
