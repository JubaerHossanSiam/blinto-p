# Role Success Plan — Rakibul

## Role

**Shopify App & Growth Lead**

**Core department:** Growth (Sales & Marketing)  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Lead Blinto's Shopify app growth work by connecting product understanding, market insight, execution, and measurable growth outcomes for internal and client app initiatives.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Growth ownership** — growth initiatives have clear hypotheses, priorities, owners, execution plans, and measurable outcomes.
2. **Shopify ecosystem expertise** — decisions reflect strong understanding of Shopify app founders, app-market dynamics, merchant needs, competitive positioning, and growth channels.
3. **Experimentation** — growth ideas are converted into practical tests, measured, documented, and iterated based on evidence.
4. **Cross-functional execution** — product, design, development, content, marketing, and support work are aligned around the most important app-growth priorities.
5. **Business impact** — work contributes to meaningful outcomes such as qualified leads, installs, activation, retention, reviews, revenue, partnerships, or stronger market authority.
6. **Reusable capability** — successful methods become repeatable playbooks, assets, dashboards, research, or systems Blinto can reuse across Shopify app work.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include experiment results, app metrics, campaign outcomes, customer or founder insights, documented growth plans, successful cross-functional launches, qualified pipeline, content or authority assets, and repeatable growth processes.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Rakibul consistently identifies high-leverage opportunities, converts insight into disciplined execution, creates measurable growth impact, and strengthens Blinto's Shopify app expertise and reusable growth capability.

**Effective** performance means priorities are clear, initiatives are executed reliably, learning is documented, stakeholders stay aligned, and work contributes meaningfully to agreed growth goals.

**Needs Improvement** applies when initiatives lack clear hypotheses or follow-through, execution becomes reactive, learning is not captured, cross-functional alignment is weak, or activity does not connect to measurable business outcomes.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
