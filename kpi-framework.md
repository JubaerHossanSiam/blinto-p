# Blinto 100-Point KPI Framework

| # | KPI | Points | Measurement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Delivery & Reliability | 10 | Task |
| 2 | Work Quality | 10 | Task |
| 3 | Ownership | 10 | Task |
| 4 | Communication | 10 | Task |
| 5 | Problem Solving | 10 | Task |
| 6 | Collaboration | 10 | Task |
| 7 | Proactiveness | 10 | Task |
| 8 | Business / Client Impact | 10 | Task + Results |
| 9 | Growth & Development | 10 | Monthly KPI Review |
| 10 | Role Excellence | 10 | Monthly KPI Review |
| | **TOTAL** | **100** | |

## Scoring

The first eight KPIs are primarily measured from task-level evidence collected during the month.

KPI 9 and KPI 10 are assessed during the monthly KPI Review because they require broader evidence and role-specific judgment.

The framework intentionally avoids asking managers to simply assign an arbitrary score out of 10. Observable ClickUp values should feed the calculation wherever practical.
