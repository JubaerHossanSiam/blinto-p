# KPI 09 — Growth & Development

## Points

**10 points**

## Measurement

**Monthly KPI Review dropdown, 1–5**

## Primary evaluator

Manager / reviewer

## Definition

Measures progress against 2–3 pre-agreed development goals. Evidence should include demonstrated learning, application, and improvement in actual work.

## Universal rating scale

| Rating | Score |
|---|---:|
| Exceptional | 5 |
| Strong | 4 |
| Effective | 3 |
| Needs Improvement | 2 |
| Significant Improvement Needed | 1 |

For a 10-point KPI using a five-point rating:

`Rating ÷ 5 × 10 = KPI score`

## Evidence standard

Use observable evidence from work, deliverables, outcomes, or agreed development goals. Avoid scoring based solely on personality or general impression.
