# Role Success Plan — Fatema

## Role

**Frontend Developer**

**Core department:** Engineering  
**Career level:** To be confirmed

## Role purpose

Build dependable frontend experiences from approved designs and requirements with strong attention to visual accuracy, responsiveness, maintainability, and delivery quality.

## Core success outcomes

1. **Implementation accuracy** — completed work reflects approved design and functional requirements.
2. **Responsive quality** — interfaces behave correctly across expected devices, screen sizes, and supported browsers.
3. **Code quality** — implementation is clear, maintainable, and aligned with team standards.
4. **Self-QA** — obvious UI, interaction, content, and regression issues are identified before review.
5. **Reliable execution** — progress, blockers, dependencies, and realistic timelines are communicated early.
6. **Learning application** — feedback is converted into visible improvement and repeated issues reduce over time.

## Observable evidence

Evidence may include implementation accuracy, responsive and browser quality, low preventable rework, code review feedback, successful releases, task reliability, and improvement after feedback.

## Role Excellence standard

**Exceptional** performance means Fatema consistently produces polished work, catches issues before review, grows rapidly in independence, and contributes useful improvements beyond assigned tasks.

**Effective** performance means assigned frontend work meets requirements, passes expected quality checks, is delivered reliably, and feedback is applied in later work.

**Needs Improvement** applies when preventable defects recur, rework remains high, testing is weak, blockers are surfaced late, or repeated feedback is not being incorporated.

## Monthly Role Excellence review

Role Excellence should be scored from documented monthly evidence against the outcomes above, not from general impressions. The universal five-level rating scale in the KPI framework applies.
