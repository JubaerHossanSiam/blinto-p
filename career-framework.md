Salary bands are gross monthly ranges and should be reviewed periodically against the market and Blinto's financial capacity.

## Proposed level — 2026 baseline cycle

No employee has an assigned Blinto career level yet.

For the first baseline cycle, Blinto gives each active employee a **proposed career level** before the October–December 2026 review period.

The proposal is a starting assessment position, not a confirmed level. For this transition cycle, current compensation positioning may be used as a practical baseline for the proposed level, together with role context. Leadership may propose a nearby higher level when there is a clear reason to assess the employee against that level.

October–December performance evidence, Role Success Plan evidence, scope, independence, ownership, judgment, and impact are then used to confirm or adjust the proposal.

Final levels are communicated by **December 31, 2026** and become active from **January 1, 2027**.

## Salary progression

Career level and compensation are connected but remain separate decisions.

- A confirmed level establishes the applicable salary band.
- Salary may progress within that band based on performance, role maturity, internal equity, market position, and company capacity.
- A KPI score does not automatically produce a salary increase.
- A proposed level does not create an immediate salary entitlement before final confirmation and compensation review.

## Dual Career Track

At L4 and above, employees may grow through two paths:

- Individual Contributor (IC Track)
- Management Track

Both tracks are equal in level and salary band.

## Role Success Plans

Every full-time role should have a Role Success Plan defining what excellent performance means for that role.

Role Excellence in the monthly KPI review is assessed against that role-specific plan.
