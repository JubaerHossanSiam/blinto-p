## Proposed level — 2026 baseline cycle

No employee has an assigned Blinto career level yet.

For the first baseline cycle, Blinto gives each active employee a **proposed career level** before the October 1–December 10, 2026 review period.

The proposal is a starting assessment position, not a confirmed level. For this transition cycle, current compensation positioning may be used as a practical baseline for the proposed level, together with role context. Leadership may propose a nearby higher level when there is a clear reason to assess the employee against that level.

October 1–December 10 performance evidence, Role Success Plan evidence, scope, independence, ownership, judgment, and impact are then used to confirm or adjust the proposal.

Final levels are announced on **December 16, 2026 (Victory Day)** and become active from **January 1, 2027**.

## Career level confirmation benchmark

The Career Level Success % measures how strongly an employee demonstrates the expectations of the level being assessed. It is separate from the monthly KPI score.

To confirm a proposed level or qualify for promotion to the next level, the employee must meet both conditions:

1. **At least 85% overall Career Level Success** against the proposed level.
2. **No critical career dimension below 70%.**

The decision guide is:

| Career Level Success | Assessment | Decision Guide |
|---:|---|---|
| 95–100% | Strongly operating at level | Confirm / promote; strong positioning within the band |
| 90–94% | Clearly operating at level | Confirm / promote |
| 85–89% | Meets proposed level | Eligible to confirm / promote |
| 75–84% | Approaching proposed level | Not yet; define development priorities |
| Below 75% | Material gaps remain | Remain at current level or reassess proposed level |

Meeting 85% makes an employee **eligible** for confirmation or promotion; it does not make the decision automatic. Leadership calibration may consider evidence quality, sustained performance, role scope, organizational need, internal equity, and company capacity.

A strong monthly KPI score does not by itself prove readiness for a higher career level. For example, an employee may be an excellent L2 performer while still developing the scope, independence, judgment, or impact required for L3.

## Annual decision calendar

- **December 10:** first-cycle evidence cutoff; use completed October–November KPI reviews plus supporting evidence from December 1–10.
- **December 11–15:** complete assessment, leadership calibration, salary review, and employee discussions.
- **December 16 (Victory Day):** announce career-level, promotion, and salary-review decisions.
- **January 1:** confirmed levels and approved changes take effect.

Annual career assessments and salary reviews follow the December 16 announcement and January 1 effective-date schedule. A review does not guarantee promotion or a salary increase. The full December KPI review continues after month-end.

## Salary progression

Career level and compensation are connected but remain separate decisions.

- A confirmed level establishes the applicable salary band.
- Career Level Success helps determine the employee's appropriate position within that band.
- Salary may also reflect role maturity, internal equity, market position, sustained performance, and company capacity.
- A KPI score does not automatically produce a salary increase.
- A proposed level does not create an immediate salary entitlement before final confirmation and compensation review.

### Salary positioning within a confirmed band

For a newly confirmed or promoted level, use Career Level Success as the starting positioning guide:

| Career Level Success | Suggested Position in Salary Band |
|---:|---|
| 85–89% | Lower band — approximately 0–35% through the range |
| 90–94% | Lower-middle to midpoint — approximately 35–60% through the range |
| 95–100% | Midpoint to upper-middle — approximately 60–80% through the range |

The top 20% of a salary band is normally reserved for employees who have demonstrated sustained high performance and maturity at that level over time and are approaching readiness for the next level.

Salary positioning is therefore a structured compensation decision, not a direct mathematical conversion of the assessment score. The Career Level Success result establishes the recommended zone; leadership then confirms the final salary using internal equity, market data, role context, and Blinto's financial capacity.

## Dual Career Track

At L4 and above, employees may grow through two paths:

- Individual Contributor (IC Track)
- Management Track

Both tracks are equal in level and salary band.

## Role Success Plans

Every full-time role should have a Role Success Plan defining what excellent performance means for that role.

Role Excellence in the monthly KPI review is assessed against that role-specific plan.
